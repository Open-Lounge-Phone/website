---
title: Pair a phone
description: Connect a phone (hardware or emulator) to your household with a six-digit code.
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

Every phone has its own cryptographic key, generated on the phone and never shared. Pairing tells
your server which household that key belongs to.

1. **Power the phone** and connect it to the internet (the emulator is already online). No
   hardware yet? Use an old phone or tablet: open `<your server>/device/` in its browser, choose
   **Add to Home Screen**, open it from there and pick **Kids phone**, **My own phone** or
   [**Lounge phone**](/how-to/lounge-phone/). Tap the big **Lift handset** button in step 2.
2. **Lift the handset.** An unpaired phone reads a six-digit code aloud through the handset
   and, if it has a display, shows it too. The code is valid for 10 minutes.
3. **In the companion app** choose **+ Pair a phone** and enter the code. The app shows **four
   words** — check the phone shows the same ones under **MENU**, then **0 About**. Different
   words mean it's a different phone: don't pair it.
4. Pick **how it will be used** (what the phone was set up as is preselected):
   - **Kids phone** — for a child: only the people you allow, quiet hours (guardians, in a home);
   - **My own phone** — a desk or bedside phone that is always you (anyone can add their own);
   - **Lounge phone** — shared; idle until someone signs in (guardians).
5. Give it a name, for example "Maya's phone". It comes **online** in the app; its strip shows
   whose phone it is ("KIDS: SMITH HOME", "JESSE'S PHONE").

On a kids' phone you're added automatically as its first contact, on speed-dial key **1**, and
you can always ring through quiet hours.

The phone's **Manage** page shows when it was last seen, the software it runs and its four words.

:::note
**Remove and wipe** (on the **Manage** page) makes the server forget the phone, and the phone
wipes itself — at once if it's online, otherwise when it next connects. Nothing stays on it. To
use a phone another way or give it to another household, remove it and pair it again.
:::
