A patient's mobile runs flat, gets lost in the bedside drawer, or is too much to manage. A
bedside phone with a handset and a few keys is easier, and it shouldn't keep anything once the
patient goes home.

## What it does

- **Use it as yourself.** The bedside phone is a [Lounge phone](/how-to/lounge-phone/): the
  patient (or a visitor helping them) scans its code and presses the key it flashes. Their own
  people are now on the keys, and calls to them ring at the bedside.
- **It forgets them.** The session ends when they log out, after the time the ward sets, at the
  end of the day, or when the next patient signs in. The server keeps only who, which phone and
  when, nothing about the calls.
- **The ward's desk, one press away.** While nobody is signed in, the keys can ring the ward's
  desk or a staff group.
- **Family anywhere.** Patients with an account on the hospital's server, another server or the
  free hub can sign in, if the hospital allows guests.

<Aside type="caution" title="Not an emergency or nurse-call system">
  Open Lounge Phone can't call emergency services and has no link to the phone network. It isn't
  a nurse-call system or a medical device: it's for keeping in touch with family and friends.
</Aside>

## Start

1. [Get started](/getting-started/) with an **Organization** space.
2. [Set up a Lounge phone](/how-to/lounge-phone/) at the bedside, choose the session length and
   turn on guests if you want them.
3. Under **Lounge settings → When nobody is signed in**, put the ward's desk on the keys.
