Not everyone wants a smartphone. A desk phone with real keys and a proper handset is easy to
use, always in the same place, and always charged.

## What it does

- **One press to call family.** Pair the phone as **My own phone** for the person who uses it,
  and their family's names sit on the digit keys: lift the handset, press 1 for Ana.
- **Only people they know.** Calls come only from their own household and the connections they
  (or whoever helps them set it up) have accepted. No phone numbers, so no robocalls and no scams by phone.
- **Voicemail they can hear on the phone.** Missed calls show on the screen; with the handset
  up, MENU, 2, then 1 plays new messages.
- **The grandkids can call.** A kid's phone in another household reaches them through the
  families' connections, with the kid's guardians deciding.

## Daily check-in (labs)

The [labs firmware](/extend/labs/) adds a check-in mode: each morning they press key 1, "I'm OK".
If nobody presses it by the time you set (say 10:00), the family gets an alert in their
voicemail inbox ("Grandma didn't check in by 10:00."), and their own phones show it as missed.
The phone can also show reminders, like `08:30 PILLS`, until a key is pressed.

The check-in has been tested on a computer, not yet on a real board. It needs the phone to be
connected to a server: your own or the free hub.

## Start

1. [Get started](/getting-started/) and create an account for them, or invite them to your
   household.
2. [Pair a phone](/how-to/pair-a-phone/) as **My own phone**.
3. Connect them with family under **Connections**, and set up [voicemail](/how-to/voicemail/).
