Many residents don't want, or can't manage, a smartphone. A desk phone with real keys and a
proper handset is easy to use, always in the same place, and always charged.

## What it does

- **Family on the keys.** Each resident's phone is their own: lift the handset, press 1 for
  their daughter. Their family calls them from the companion app on any phone or computer.
- **Only people they know.** Calls come only from people the resident (or whoever helps them)
  has accepted. No phone numbers, so no robocalls and no scam calls.
- **Reception, by extension.** MENU, 6 and the front desk's number rings the staff on duty as a
  ring group: whoever answers first takes it. A key can also call a member of staff directly.
- **Voicemail on the handset.** Missed calls show on the screen, and messages play in the handset.
- **A daily check-in (labs).** The [labs firmware](/extend/labs/) can ask a resident to press
  "I'm OK" each morning and alert their family if they don't. It has been tested on a computer,
  not yet on a real board.

<Aside type="caution" title="Not an emergency or nurse-call system">
  Open Lounge Phone can't call emergency services and has no link to the phone network. It isn't
  a nurse-call system or a medical device: keep the home's own alarm and call systems.
</Aside>

## Start

1. [Get started](/getting-started/) with an **Organization** space for the home, and invite each
   resident's family.
2. [Pair a phone](/how-to/pair-a-phone/) as **My own phone** for each resident, and connect them
   with their family under **Connections**.
3. Set up a ring group for reception under [extensions and ring
   groups](/how-to/workplace-phone-system/), and [voicemail](/how-to/voicemail/).
